About

GeoExplorer is a geolocation game where you can explore places around the world and test how well you know your geography. Play on your own, compete with friends, or try different maps and game modes to keep things interesting.

  • Explore random locations from around the world
  • Play on different maps and in different game modes
  • Compete with friends and other players

We only collect the information GeoExplorer needs to work properly. This includes things like your username, email, preferences, and gameplay progress. We use this information to provide the features of the site and improve your experience.
