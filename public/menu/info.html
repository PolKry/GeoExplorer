About

GeoExplorer is your modern geolocation challenge platform. Inspired by classic geoguessing gameplay but built with a sleeker interface and smoother user experience, GeoExplorer lets you test your world knowledge through randomly generated Street View locations, custom map packs, and community‑driven challenges.

GeoExplorer collects only essential data needed to operate gameplay features, save user progress, and provide community interactions. We do not sell your data to third parties.
